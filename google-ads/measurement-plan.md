# Measurement Plan

## Current economics supplied for the pilot

- Blended realisation assumption: INR 300 per litre. Internal only; do not publish as customer pricing.
- Contribution per litre: INR 157.
- Contribution margin: 52.3%.
- Break-even volume: 34.9 litres per day.

Illustrative first-order contribution:

| Litres | Contribution |
| --- | ---: |
| 1 | INR 157 |
| 2 | INR 314 |
| 3 | INR 471 |
| 4 | INR 628 |

## What must be measured before setting allowable CAC

- Average litres in the first order.
- Click-to-qualified-lead rate.
- Qualified-lead-to-order rate.
- Repeat-order rate.
- Customer lifetime litres.
- Average delivery cost by route.

Until these are known, do not optimise only for cheap clicks and do not treat the INR 157 per-litre contribution as an allowable acquisition cost.

## Commercial scorecard

- Cost per qualified lead.
- Cost per first order.
- Cost per acquired customer.
- Litres per acquired customer.
- Contribution after advertising.
- Repeat-order rate.
- Performance by public delivery corridor.

## Search-term review

Review search terms twice each week during the pilot. Classify every meaningful term as `RELEVANT`, `POTENTIALLY RELEVANT`, `IRRELEVANT` or `MEDICAL/UNSAFE`. Add irrelevant and unsafe terms as negatives. Do not automatically convert every new term into a keyword.
